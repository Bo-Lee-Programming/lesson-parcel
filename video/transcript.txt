A lesson cannot help someone who cannot reach it. Many learners share one old phone, buy data in 
small bundles, and often have no signal at home. And research shows that sending practice alone is 
not enough: learners improve when a teacher follows up. Lesson Parcel does both. The teacher writes 
a short lesson in this web page. For each wrong answer, they can add a hint about the mistake 
behind it. Download, and the whole lesson is one file of about 18 kilobytes. It goes out in the 
class chat or by Bluetooth. The learner switches on airplane mode. The lesson still opens. It can 
read aloud, and several learners can share one phone. A wrong answer gets the hint for that exact 
answer, not the solution. The explanation comes after a second try. At the end: a first-try score, 
and practice for the questions they missed. High contrast and larger text are one tap away, and the 
lesson is built for keyboards and screen readers. Then the learner gets a short code and sends it 
by plain SMS. No data needed. The teacher pastes the messages. Typos are caught, and the class 
table shows what was hard. Then the follow-up plan: learners grouped by the wrong answer they 
chose, each group with a one-SMS message about its own mistake. Copy, send, done. The teacher knows 
whom to help, and with what. Lesson Parcel: one lesson, one tiny file, and a way back to the 
teacher. Free, open source, and tested offline.
